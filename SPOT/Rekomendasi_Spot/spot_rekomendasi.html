Bayan Village & Cafe

Tourist attraction and culinary spot with an open nature concept in the Baturraden area, offering a cool mountain atmosphere with views of forests and rivers. It is suitable for hanging out, healing, and gathering with friends or family.

Location
Dusun III Kalipagu, Ketenger, Baturraden, Banyumas Regency, Central Java
Opening Hours
Everyday - 08.00 am - 22.00 pm
Price Range Bayan Resto
Rp 25.000 - Rp 150.000
Price Range Bayan Village
Starting from Rp 400.000

Bayan Village & Cafe

Bayan Village offers a variety of food and beverages that are perfect to enjoy in an open-air natural setting. The menu is quite diverse, ranging from heavy meals and light snacks to hot and refreshing drinks such as coffee, tea, and fruit juices.
